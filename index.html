    Drum sounds

    The sounds are synthesized in the browser, so you hear them a little later than your kit's own sound module (USB plus audio output latency). If you monitor through the module, turn this off.

    Latency

    This value is subtracted from every hit. Run the calibration to measure it for your setup. Bluetooth headphones usually need 150 ms or more, so use wired headphones or speakers if you can.

    Latency calibration

    You'll hear a click at 100 BPM: four count-in clicks, then hit any pad on every click about 30 times. Listen to the click and play as naturally as you can. Don't watch the screen.

    This measures the whole chain: kit → USB → browser, plus browser → speakers → your ears. Use the same headphones or speakers you practice with.
