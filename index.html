    Drum sounds

    The sounds are synthesized in the browser, so you hear them a little later than your kit's own sound module (USB plus audio output latency). If you monitor through the module, turn this off.

    Song sync

    Many osu! maps put their beats slightly ahead of the music. Auto-sync measures that for each song from its drums. If songs still feel consistently early or late to you, adjust this once (+ moves the beats later).

    Latency

    If the audio stutters or the scrolling grid jumps back during playback, increase this. A larger buffer makes the sound a little later, so run the calibration again after changing it.

    Audio buffer changed. Run the calibration again so your timing stays accurate.

    This value is subtracted from every hit. Run the calibration to measure it for your setup. Bluetooth headphones usually need 150 ms or more, so use wired headphones or speakers if you can.

    Latency calibration

    You'll hear a click at 140 BPM: four count-in clicks, then hit any pad on every click about 30 times. Listen to the click and play as naturally as you can. Don't watch the screen.

    This measures the whole chain: kit → USB → browser, plus browser → speakers → your ears. Use the same headphones or speakers you practice with.

    Add songs

    Any audio file

    MP3, OGG, WAV, …

    DrumCoach works out the tempo for you. This works best for songs with a constant tempo. Preview the song with the click track, and if the beat feels off, pick another tempo option or shift the beat until it fits.

    osu! beatmaps

    .osz files or unpacked beatmap folders

    Beatmaps come with hand-made timing, so they're more accurate and follow tempo changes, which makes them ideal for more complex songs. Browse them at osu.ppy.sh/beatmapsets or on the mirror beatconnect.io. Add the downloaded .osz files, or beatmap folders straight from your osu! Songs folder.

    • Every song you add is saved to your library in this browser, and it stays there when you leave the page.
    • You can also drag and drop files or folders anywhere on the page, and add as many at once as you like.
